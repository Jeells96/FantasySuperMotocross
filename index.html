Injury Standby

Assign a backup rider. The standby scores only for rounds where the original rider didn't race, and earns 75% of normal points (rounded).

Replacement rule: The standby must have been drafted after the injured rider, or be undrafted. Example: if your injured rider was Pick #7, the earliest pick you can choose as standby is #8.
Temporary backup only — the original rider always counts first if they raced that round.
